# House style

Voice, formatting, terminology, branding and audience defaults: none captured yet.

## Plain words

For anything that explains: a tutor reply, a lesson, a how-to.

- Define a term in the sentence that first uses it.
- One idea per sentence, about twenty words.
- One name per thing, kept for the whole document.
- Example before abstraction.
- The reader's words over the field's, until the field's word is defined.

## Templates

### Tutor reply

The reply shape of the `tutor` skill. Four moves, in order, every reply:

1. **Locate.** Quote the line or section of the source the question is about.
2. **Gap.** Name the one concept the question shows is missing. One.
3. **Build.** Explain that concept through the source's own example; the answer falls
   out of it, in this reply.
4. **Check.** One line: a question answerable from this reply alone.

Budget: prose twelve lines at most; code fifteen lines at most, and only when the source
is code; one example. A gap that is a chain: the chain in one line, link one built, link
two offered as the next question. An answer the budget cannot hold is split across
replies, each within budget.
