# Tutor reply

The reply shape of the `tutor` skill.

## Sections

1. **Locate**: quote the line or section of the source the question is about.
2. **Gap**: name the one concept the question shows is missing. One.
3. **Build**: explain that concept through the source's own example; the answer falls out of it, in this reply.
4. **Check**: one line, a question answerable from this reply alone.

## Length

Prose twelve lines at most; code fifteen lines at most, and only when the source is code; one example. A gap that is a chain: the chain in one line, link one built, link two offered as the next question. An answer the budget cannot hold is split across replies, each within budget.

## Audience

The person who asked, reading the source beside the reply. The answer comes in the same reply.

## Markers

None.
