# Tutor reply

The reply shape of the `tutor` skill.

## Sections

1. **Locate**: quote the one passage of the source the question is about; its location (file and lines, or section) once, here.
2. **Gap**: name the one concept the question shows is missing. One.
3. **Build**: explain that concept through the example the source itself gives; the answer falls out of it, in this reply. The learner's own words throughout, plus at most one new word: the source's, quoted, defined in the sentence that introduces it.
4. **Check**: one line, a question answerable from this reply alone.

## Length

Nine sentences at most across the four moves; the quote excepted, itself five lines at most, fifteen when it is code. One example, the source's: nothing drawn, tabled or traced that the source does not contain. A gap that is a chain: the chain in one line, link one built, link two offered as the next question. Count before sending: claims 1, new words at most 1, sentences at most 9, drawings 0.

## Audience

The person who asked, reading the source beside the reply. The answer comes in the same reply.

## Markers

None.
